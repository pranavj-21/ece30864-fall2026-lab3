1.
Pros of Agile Development: Ideal for innovative projects, promotes team involvement and collaboration
Cons of Agile Development: Changing Delivery Dates, Scalability Issues

2.
Product Owner: Focuses on maximizing product value and managing business priorities.
Scrum Master: Focuses on Guiding the team's processes and removing obstacles.

3.
Sprint Planning Meeting: Determines what to complete in the coming sprint, Starts Sprint
Daily Scrum: Short meetings that happen everyday, members discuss work completed
Sprint Retrospective Meeting: Discuss Work completed, close sprint

4.
Epic(top level) is the largest goal that contains smaller peer items like Stories, Tasks and Bugs.
Story(system requirement) is a feature written from the end user.
Task is a piece of work that needs to be done like a tech operation.
Bug is a flaw or error that breaks a function in a product that needs to be fixed.
